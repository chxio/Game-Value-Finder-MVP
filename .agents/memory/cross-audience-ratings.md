---
name: Cross-audience ratings
description: Why player and critic game scores are compared without averaging them
---

Keep Steam player-review positive share as the PC value-ranking basis and show Metacritic PC critic scores separately when available. Do not average the two audiences or substitute a critic score when player data is absent.

**Why:** A positive-review share is not equivalent to a critic aggregate, even after both are expressed on a five-point display scale. Combining them implies comparability that the sources do not establish.

**How to apply:** New review sources should have explicit attribution, audience, original scale, verified game identity, and link. An unavailable source should remain absent rather than become a zero or an estimated sentiment score.
